# Refund Policy

This article describes our refund policy for subscription charges and explains how to request a refund.

## Standard Refund Window

We offer a **30-day money-back guarantee** on all new subscriptions. If you are not satisfied with Acme SaaS within the first 30 days of your initial subscription, contact us and we will issue a full refund — no questions asked.

After the 30-day window, we do not issue prorated refunds for unused time in the current billing period. However, we may make exceptions in the following circumstances:

- A verified billing error (e.g., you were charged twice)
- Significant service outage during your billing period (see SLA policy)
- Account access was compromised through no fault of your own

## How to Request a Refund

1. Email billing@acme-saas.com from the email address associated with your account.
2. Include your account name, the charge date, and the reason for your request.
3. Our billing team will respond within one business day.

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method. Timeframes depend on your bank or card issuer: typically 5–10 business days for credit cards, 3–5 business days for ACH transfers.

## Annual Plan Refunds

If you purchased an annual plan and cancel within the first 30 days, you receive a full refund. After 30 days, we refund the unused months on a prorated basis, minus any discounts applied because of the annual commitment.

## Add-On Purchases

One-time add-ons (such as additional storage or onboarding sessions) are non-refundable unless there was a service failure on our part.

## Disputes and Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact us before filing a dispute with your bank. Chargebacks incur processing fees that cannot be waived. We can almost always resolve billing issues faster and more smoothly by working directly with you.

## Contact

For any billing question, reach out to billing@acme-saas.com or call our billing hotline at +1 (800) 555-0192, available Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM Eastern.
