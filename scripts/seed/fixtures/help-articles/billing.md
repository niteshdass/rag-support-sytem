# Billing, Invoices, and Plan Management

This article explains how billing works, how to manage your subscription, and how to access your invoices.

## Plans Overview

Acme SaaS offers three plans:

| Plan | Monthly Price | Agents | Knowledge Sources | Auto-Resolve |
|------|--------------|--------|-------------------|--------------|
| Starter | $49 | Up to 5 | 3 | No |
| Pro | $149 | Up to 20 | Unlimited | Yes |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited | Yes + SLA |

All plans are billed monthly by default. Annual billing is available at a 20% discount.

## Changing Your Plan

To upgrade or downgrade, go to **Settings > Billing > Change Plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

If you need to add more agent seats mid-cycle without changing your plan tier, go to **Settings > Billing > Add Seats**. Each additional seat is billed at your plan's per-seat rate, prorated for the remainder of the current cycle.

## Payment Methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. We also support ACH bank transfers for annual Enterprise contracts. To add or update a payment method, go to **Settings > Billing > Payment Methods**.

If a payment fails, we retry the charge after 3 days and again after 7 days. After three failed attempts, we send a warning email and limit API access. Your data is never deleted due to a billing issue — we'll contact you to resolve it first.

## Invoices

All invoices are available at **Settings > Billing > Invoices**. You can download PDF copies for your records. Invoices are issued on the same day each month (the day you first subscribed). To add a billing email address or purchase order number to your invoices, go to **Settings > Billing > Invoice Settings**.

## Cancellation

To cancel, go to **Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription**. You can continue using Acme until the end of your current billing period. After cancellation, you have 30 days to export your data before it is permanently deleted.

## Tax and VAT

If you are a VAT-registered business in the EU, add your VAT ID in **Settings > Billing > Tax Information** to receive tax-exempt invoices.

For billing questions not covered here, contact billing@acme-saas.com.
