# Using the Acme SaaS Mobile App

The Acme SaaS mobile app lets support agents manage tickets and query the knowledge base from their phones. Available on iOS 16+ and Android 10+.

## Downloading the App

- **iOS**: Search "Acme SaaS" in the App Store, or use [this link].
- **Android**: Search "Acme SaaS" in Google Play, or use [this link].

Scan the QR code under **Settings > Mobile App** to get a download link sent to your phone.

## Logging In

Open the app and enter your workspace URL (e.g., `yourcompany.acme-saas.com`) and your credentials. If your organization uses SSO, tap **Sign in with SSO** and follow your company's login flow. Touch ID / Face ID can be enabled after the first login under **App Settings > Biometrics**.

## Managing Tickets on Mobile

The **Inbox** tab shows all tickets assigned to you, sorted by priority. You can:

- **Reply** to a ticket using the text editor or dictate a response using your device's keyboard microphone.
- **Reassign** to another agent or queue.
- **Change status** (Open, Pending, Resolved, Closed).
- **Add notes** visible only to agents.
- **Apply macros** with a single tap.

AI-drafted replies are available on mobile the same as on desktop. Tap the **AI Draft** button in the reply toolbar to generate a suggested response based on the customer's message and your knowledge base.

## Push Notifications

Push notifications are enabled by default. You receive notifications for:

- New ticket assigned to you
- Customer reply on an open ticket
- Mention in a ticket comment (`@yourname`)
- Escalation from auto-resolve

Adjust notification preferences under **App Settings > Notifications**. You can set quiet hours to pause notifications (e.g., 10 PM–7 AM).

## Querying the Knowledge Base

Tap the search icon (magnifying glass) from any screen to query the knowledge base. Type your question in natural language. The app returns an AI-generated answer with cited sources, identical to the desktop experience.

## Offline Mode

The app caches your 50 most recent tickets and the 100 most recently viewed knowledge base articles for offline viewing. Edits made offline sync automatically when your connection is restored.

## Troubleshooting

- **Notifications not arriving**: Check that notifications are enabled in your device's system settings for the Acme app.
- **Sync issues**: Pull down on the ticket list to force a refresh, or log out and back in.
- **Biometrics not working after phone upgrade**: Re-enable under **App Settings > Biometrics**.

For other issues, use the **Report a Problem** option under **App Settings** to send a diagnostic log to our mobile team.
