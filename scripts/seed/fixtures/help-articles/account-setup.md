# Getting Started: Setting Up Your Account

Welcome to Acme SaaS. This guide walks you through everything you need to do after signing up to get your workspace fully operational.

## Step 1: Verify Your Email

After registration, we send a verification email to the address you provided. Click the **Verify Email** link inside it. The link expires in 24 hours. If you do not receive the email within five minutes, check your spam folder. To resend the verification email, go to **Account Settings > Profile > Resend Verification**.

## Step 2: Complete Your Profile

Navigate to **Account Settings > Profile** and fill in:

- **Full name** — used in notifications and audit logs
- **Display name** — shown to teammates
- **Time zone** — affects how timestamps and scheduled reports appear
- **Notification preferences** — choose between email, in-app, or both

## Step 3: Set Up Two-Factor Authentication

We strongly recommend enabling 2FA before inviting teammates. Go to **Account Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication** and follow the setup wizard. We support authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password) and hardware keys (YubiKey, FIDO2).

## Step 4: Invite Your Team

Go to **Settings > Team Members > Invite**. Enter email addresses, assign roles, and click **Send Invites**. Invited users receive an email with a join link valid for 72 hours. Roles available:

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Admin | Full access including billing and user management |
| Manager | Can manage agents and view all tickets |
| Agent | Can view and respond to tickets in their assigned queues |
| Viewer | Read-only access to dashboards and reports |

## Step 5: Connect Your First Integration

After your team is in, connect Acme to your existing tools. Go to **Integrations** and choose from Zendesk, Intercom, Slack, Notion, or a help center URL. Each connector has a step-by-step OAuth flow. Most take under two minutes.

## Need Help?

If you run into trouble during setup, open a chat from any page using the widget in the bottom-right corner, or email support@acme-saas.com. Our team typically responds within two business hours.
